## 🚀 About Me

I lead the team behind **SAFEJETS** — quality and safety management products used by
business-aviation operators. In this domain a bug isn't a bug, it's a compliance finding,
and that changes how you build.

Before that I **founded my own company in 2019** and ran it as CTO for three years, shipping
two cryptocurrency-exchange products. After military service and a move to Türkiye, I started
again as an engineer and was leading the team within a year.

I work **forward-deployed**: sitting close to the operators who actually use what we build,
shipping against their live workflows instead of a spec written three months earlier.

I care about:
- architecture that survives growth
- code that explains itself
- systems that don't panic under load
